Pauza

Download your tachograph driver card on your phone and see driving time left, rests and infringements.

Read only, never changes your card

Pauza only sends read commands to the card. Every download is checked against the card's signature and the EU tachograph keys, and the original file is kept unchanged.

What you see

  • Driving time left until your next break, today, this week and this fortnight
  • When your next daily and weekly rest is due
  • Infringements of the EU driving and rest rules (Regulation 561/2006 and the Mobility Package), graded by seriousness
  • Shifts with exact start and end times, in your local time
  • Hours worked per week and month, kilometres driven
  • Your whole history: every download is added, nothing is lost
  • Live driving times from a Bluetooth smart tachograph (Gen2v2)
  • Excel history and Google Drive copies in two taps

Your data stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

What you need

  • A driver card (Gen1 or Gen2 smart tachograph card)
  • A USB smart card reader that plugs into your phone (USB-C, or with an adapter)
