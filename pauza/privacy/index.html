Pauza privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Pauza (“the app”) is made by Piti Prazelion, Spain (contact: valy528@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: your data stays on your phone. The app has no account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us.

What the app reads

  • Your driver card, through a USB card reader you plug into the phone. The card holds your name, date of birth, card and driving licence numbers, your driving, work, availability and rest times, the vehicles you drove (registration numbers and kilometres), the places you started and ended your days, and on smart tachograph (Gen2) cards, positions recorded by the tachograph. The app only reads the card; it never changes anything on it.
  • The tachograph in your vehicle, if you use the Bluetooth live link: your current activity, driving times and speed, as the tachograph reports them.

Where it is kept

On a car screen (Android Auto), the app shows your remaining driving times; nothing is sent to the car maker or to us.

Everything above is stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not backed up to the cloud by the app, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

When it leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Google Drive or another app: when you tap “Save to Google Drive” or “Save a copy”, Android’s share screen hands the files to the app you pick. What happens then is covered by that app’s own privacy policy.
  • Your own PC (optional): if you enter the address of your own Pauza program on your computer, card downloads are sent to that computer over your network. We never receive them.

The app does not send any data to its maker or to any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Permissions

  • USB: to talk to the card reader.
  • Bluetooth (and, on Android 11 and older, location, which Android requires for Bluetooth scanning): to find and connect to your tachograph for the live link. The app does not use your location.
  • Notifications and a foreground service: to keep the live link to the tachograph running with the screen off, with a notification you can stop it from.
  • Internet: the app’s dashboard runs inside the app on the phone itself (127.0.0.1), and the optional sending to your own PC uses your network.

Children

The app is meant for professional drivers and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we do not receive or hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control all of it on your phone: uninstalling the app removes all of it. If you have questions, write to valy528@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
